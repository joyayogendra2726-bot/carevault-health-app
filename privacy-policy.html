Privacy Policy for CareVault: Family Meds & Vitals

Effective Date: November 2026

Zero Data Collection: CareVault is a 100% offline application. All medication schedules, family profiles, vital logs, and emergency notes stay strictly on your own device.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal, medical, or health information on external servers. There are no user accounts, no cloud syncing, and no third-party advertising SDKs.

2. Permissions Used

  • Notifications & Exact Alarms: Used solely to trigger local medication reminders on your device.
  • Google Play Billing: In-app purchases and subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play. We do not access or store your payment details.

3. Medical & Wellness Disclaimer

CareVault is a personal wellness tracking and medication reminder tool. It does not provide medical diagnosis, clinical advice, or treatment recommendations. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical decisions.
